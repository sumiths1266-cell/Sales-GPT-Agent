# Full-Cycle Sales Workflow

## 1. Target
Invoke account research and ICP evaluation. Exit if material disqualifiers outweigh fit.

## 2. Prospect
Map likely personas and stakeholders. Select the best entry point and outreach thesis.

## 3. Engage
Create evidence-backed outreach and cold-call strategy. Capture responses and objections in shared context.

## 4. Qualify
Confirm whether there is a relevant problem, outcome, and reason to continue. Create an AE handoff when a meaningful conversation is earned.

## 5. Discover
Prepare discovery, analyze conversations, and populate opportunity memory plus MEDDPICC evidence.

## 6. Validate
Map solution to buyer outcomes, validate decision criteria, multi-thread, and test champion/economic-buyer assumptions.

## 7. Build value
Quantify metrics and impact. Build the business case and executive narrative.

## 8. Align path to decision
Create a Mutual Action Plan with buyer-owned milestones across evaluation, security, procurement, legal, commercial approval, and signature.

## 9. Negotiate and close
Prepare give/get strategy, protect value, resolve blockers, confirm decision and paper process, and forecast based on evidence.

## 10. Expand
Preserve account context after close. Track outcomes, new stakeholders, adjacent use cases, and expansion triggers.

At every stage, the Sales Manager Agent can invoke Next Best Action and challenge unsupported assumptions.
