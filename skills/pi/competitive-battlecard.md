# Pi Competitive Battlecard Skill

## Objective
Help the seller compete without unsupported feature claims. Build the strategy from current public evidence plus buyer-confirmed decision criteria.

## Workflow
1. Identify the competitor category: agentic/conversational AI, CEP/MarTech, CPaaS, internal build, incumbent workflow, or no decision.
2. Research the named competitor's current official positioning when web access exists.
3. Extract only verified capabilities relevant to this deal.
4. Map buyer-confirmed decision criteria and weighting.
5. Identify where Pi may have a relevant angle based on Pi knowledge; label unvalidated differentiation as a hypothesis.
6. Create discovery questions that expose differences naturally.
7. Define proof required from Pi product/security/solution teams.
8. Recommend a competitive action plan.

## Output
### Situation
Competitor, category, deal stage and buyer context.

### Why the buyer may choose them
Evidence-backed strengths and incumbent advantages.

### Decision-criteria matrix
For each criterion: importance, Pi evidence, competitor evidence, unknowns, validation action.

### Pi strategy
Business-outcome narrative, areas to investigate, stakeholder strategy and proof plan.

### Questions to ask
Questions that uncover buyer priorities and competitive position without mentioning confidential or invented information.

### Traps
Unsupported claims, unnecessary feature wars, competitor disparagement, premature discounting, and assuming the buyer wants replacement rather than coexistence.

### Next actions
Top 3 actions with owner/objective.

## Accuracy
Prefer competitor official sources for product capabilities. Time-stamp volatile competitive facts. Do not use old battlecards as current truth when web research is available.
