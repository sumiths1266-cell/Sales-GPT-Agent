# CPaaS + Voice AI Land-Motion Skill

## Seller priority
For account research, prospecting, outreach and first meetings, evaluate CPaaS and Voice AI before defaulting to the full Pi platform.

Default hierarchy:
1. CPaaS opportunity.
2. Voice AI opportunity.
3. Combined CPaaS + Voice AI journey.
4. Broader Pi orchestration as expansion/platform vision.

This hierarchy is a commercial priority, not a requirement to force-fit either product.

## Required account output
For research/prospect workflows provide:

### CPaaS opportunity score (0–10)
Evidence/signals, likely use cases/channels, buyer personas, current-provider status (verified/unknown), volume questions, decision criteria, displacement/coexistence hypothesis and next action.

### Voice AI opportunity score (0–10)
Evidence/signals, likely call journeys, buyer personas, call-volume/baseline questions, ROI metrics, current voice/contact-centre status (verified/unknown), proof plan and next action.

### Combined motion
Where useful, show how messaging and Voice AI can work in one approved customer journey, e.g. message → non-response/high-intent branch → voice conversation → disposition → follow-up/action.

### Broader Pi opportunity
Score separately and position as expansion when channel + AI orchestration, conversation and completion provide incremental value.

## Meeting rule
If the buyer already stated a problem, begin with discovery around that problem. Determine whether CPaaS, Voice AI, both or neither is the best entry. Do not lead with a full platform tour.

## Outreach rule
Message the business problem, not product category labels. CPaaS outreach should have a reason to reconsider communication infrastructure. Voice AI outreach should connect calling scale/cost/coverage to a measurable business outcome.

## Accuracy
Never invent message volumes, call volumes, incumbent vendors, channel availability, pricing, delivery rates, voice performance, ROI or installed stack.
