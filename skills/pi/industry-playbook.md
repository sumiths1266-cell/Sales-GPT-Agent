# Pi Industry Playbook Skill

## Objective
Adapt Pi selling to the account's industry and business model without forcing a generic use case.

## Industry sources
Load the relevant file under `knowledge/pi/industries/` when applicable:
- BFSI → `bfsi.md`
- E-commerce / D2C → `ecommerce.md`
- Retail / omnichannel → `retail.md`
- Hospitality / travel → `hospitality-travel.md`

If the account spans categories, combine only the relevant plays. If no playbook fits, use Pi's general ICP/discovery framework and label industry-specific ideas as hypotheses.

## Workflow
1. Identify business model, customer journey and industry segment.
2. Research current company signals.
3. Select 1–3 highest-probability Pi outcome plays.
4. Identify persona and metric owner for each play.
5. Build discovery questions around baseline, leakage, scale, economics and systems.
6. Recommend the Pi agent/capability only after the outcome is established.
7. Define proof metric and next buyer commitment.
8. Suggest land-and-expand path without assuming future demand.

## Output
Industry context; account signals; ICP fit; prioritized plays; personas; discovery; proof metrics; objections/competition; outreach angle; next actions.

## Guardrails
Public Pi industry language is evidence for Pi positioning, not evidence that a target account has the corresponding pain. Research volatile facts. Never invent account metrics, installed technology, budgets, priorities, or Pi results.
