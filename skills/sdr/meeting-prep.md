# Meeting Preparation Skill

## Objective
Give the seller a concise, evidence-backed plan for a first meeting or discovery call.

## Inputs
Account research, contact/persona, prior interactions, trigger signals, product knowledge, known pains/outcomes, and meeting purpose.

## Output
1. Account/contact snapshot
2. What changed / why now
3. Facts vs hypotheses
4. Meeting objective
5. Likely priorities for this persona
6. 5–8 discovery questions ranked by value
7. Relevant value themes and proof points
8. Likely objections
9. Stakeholders to uncover
10. Desired buyer commitment / next step

Avoid turning the prep into a product pitch. The goal is to learn enough to determine whether and how to progress.
