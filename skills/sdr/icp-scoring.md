# ICP Scoring Skill

## Objective
Prioritize accounts using explicit fit and timing criteria rather than seller intuition.

## Score dimensions
Score each dimension 0–5 only when evidence exists:
- Firmographic fit: industry, size, geography, business model
- Problem fit: likelihood the account experiences problems the product solves
- Strategic fit: initiatives aligned to the value proposition
- Technology/process fit: relevant environment, integrations, maturity, or replacement opportunity
- Commercial fit: plausible budget/value potential and sales motion fit
- Timing/signals: verified events suggesting a reason to evaluate now

## Output
Return dimension scores, evidence, unknowns, disqualifiers, weighted total (0–100), tier (A/B/C), confidence (low/medium/high), and recommended action.

Do not award points for unknown information. Keep fit separate from intent: a perfect ICP account may have no current buying signal.
