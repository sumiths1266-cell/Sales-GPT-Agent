# Full Account Dossier & Outreach Execution Skill

## Objective
When the seller asks to research an account, do not stop at company facts or generic personas. Produce an outreach-ready enterprise prospecting dossier that answers: Why this account? What Pi use case? Who exactly should I contact? Why them? What do I say? What should I do next?

## Required research depth
Use current web research for volatile account and prospect facts. Prefer company websites, annual reports, investor presentations, regulatory filings, leadership pages, reputable business news, conference/interview sources, and public LinkedIn pages/search results where accessible.

Never invent a person's name, title, LinkedIn URL, employment status, email address, phone number, quote, post, or priority.

## Output contract

### 1. Executive account brief
- What the company does, scale, segments/geographies and business model relevant to Pi.
- Current strategic priorities and recent material triggers.
- Existing digital/customer-engagement/AI/channel capabilities.
- Sources and dates for volatile facts.

### 2. Pi ICP score
Score 0–100 with evidence for customer/conversation scale, measurable commercial journeys, channel complexity, AI/digital maturity, likely business value and timing signals.

### 3. Prioritized Pi opportunity map
Select 3–5 plays maximum. For each: business problem hypothesis, evidence/trigger, relevant Pi capability/channel orchestration, buyer KPI, discovery metric and confidence. Separate evidence from hypothesis.

### 4. Buying committee map
Map business owner, operational owner, likely economic buyer, champion candidate, technical/data/security validators and procurement where relevant. Persona labels are not enough: discover actual people when public evidence exists.

### 5. Named prospect list
Target 5–10 relevant people when verifiable. For each provide:
- name;
- current title;
- role in likely buying process;
- why this person matters for the selected Pi play;
- public LinkedIn profile URL only if directly verified;
- other useful public source;
- confidence/currentness.
If the exact person or profile cannot be verified, say `Not verified` and provide a LinkedIn/Sales Navigator search string rather than fabricating a URL.

### 6. Primary prospect deep research
Choose the strongest 1–3 contacts. Research their remit, tenure/background when relevant, public interviews/posts/speeches, initiatives, stated priorities and likely KPI ownership. Distinguish direct evidence from inference.

### 7. Personalized messaging
Create messaging from account + prospect evidence, not generic persona templates.
For the primary prospect provide:
- cold-call opener and 30-second reason for call;
- LinkedIn connection note;
- LinkedIn follow-up after acceptance;
- Email 1: trigger/problem hypothesis;
- Email 2: business outcome/use case;
- Email 3: useful follow-up/close-the-loop;
- optional WhatsApp message only when the seller has a legitimate business contact route and it is appropriate.
Keep outreach concise, human and outcome-led. Do not pretend familiarity.

### 8. Multi-thread strategy
Explain who to contact first, second and third; what distinct hypothesis to use with each; and how to avoid sending identical messaging across the committee.

### 9. Discovery preparation
Provide 8–12 account-specific discovery questions covering baseline, leakage, volume, current channels, orchestration, systems, economics, decision process, security/compliance and success criteria.

### 10. Competitive/status-quo hypothesis
Identify likely internal build, CPaaS, CEP/MarTech, conversational AI or existing workflow alternatives only where evidence supports them. Never invent installed vendors.

### 11. Outreach sequence
Create a practical 7–10 business-day sequence across call, LinkedIn and email. Every touch should add a reason, insight or question; do not repeat the same copy.

### 12. Seller action plan
End with the top 3 actions the seller should execute now.

## Prospect verification rules
A LinkedIn search result/snippet is evidence that a profile likely exists but may not prove the exact current role. Prefer direct public profile/company leadership evidence. If identity is ambiguous, label it ambiguous. Do not guess LinkedIn slugs.

## Pi rule
Sell customer journeys and measurable outcomes across channels. Do not reduce Pi to Voice AI or WhatsApp APIs. Respect current-vs-roadmap channel status from Pi knowledge.
