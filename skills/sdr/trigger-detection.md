# Trigger Detection Skill

## Objective
Find verified changes that may create a timely reason for a sales conversation.

## Trigger classes
Leadership changes, funding, M&A, geographic expansion, product launches, hiring patterns, layoffs/restructuring, technology changes, regulatory changes, strategic announcements, partnerships, earnings commentary, operational incidents, and relevant competitive moves.

## Analysis
For each trigger capture: event, date, source/evidence, affected business area, plausible implication, relevant persona, value hypothesis, confidence, and shelf life.

## Rules
A trigger is not intent. Never say an account is buying because an event occurred. Translate events into testable hypotheses and rank by relevance + recency + business impact.

## Output
Top triggers, why each matters, hypothesis to test, persona to approach, suggested opening angle, and whether the trigger changes account priority.
