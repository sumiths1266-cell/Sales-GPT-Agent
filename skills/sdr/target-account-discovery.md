# Target Account Discovery — Signal-Based Territory Intelligence

## Objective
Find and rank companies that are strong near-term targets for the seller's current land motions: CPaaS and Voice AI. Use current public web/news evidence to discover accounts, not just research companies already known to the seller.

## Research philosophy
Search broadly across public company sites, annual reports, investor presentations, regulatory filings, business/news publications, funding/IPO/M&A news, hiring pages, product launches, app/digital initiatives, customer-experience initiatives, expansion announcements, contact-centre/collections/growth initiatives and relevant public executive commentary.

Do not claim to have crawled or scraped the entire internet. Work from accessible public web/search evidence. Respect source access restrictions. Prefer recent primary/high-quality sources for volatile claims.

## Discovery universe
Prioritize India and seller-relevant enterprise segments unless the seller specifies another geography. Strong categories include BFSI, lending/NBFC, fintech/payments, insurance, e-commerce/marketplaces, D2C, retail, quick commerce, travel/hospitality, mobility, consumer internet, healthcare, education and other businesses with large customer communication or calling volumes.

## Trigger library
Look for signals such as:
- rapid customer/user/merchant growth;
- geographic/store/branch/category expansion;
- funding, IPO preparation or post-funding growth investment;
- launch of lending/cards/insurance/subscription/loyalty products;
- large collections/renewal/retention operations;
- customer-service/contact-centre expansion;
- CRM/lifecycle/growth/CX/collections hiring;
- WhatsApp/RCS/SMS/customer-messaging initiatives;
- AI/voice/contact-centre automation initiatives;
- dormant-user/reactivation/retention focus;
- high COD/RTO/order-status/customer-notification needs;
- high lead volumes or application abandonment;
- regulatory/service changes that create customer-communication volume;
- mergers/acquisitions/platform migrations that create consolidation needs.

A trigger is a reason to investigate, not proof of pain or purchase intent.

## Opportunity scoring (100)
Score each account with evidence:
- CPaaS fit: 0–25 — likely communication scale, multi-channel need, transactional/engagement messaging, API/platform fit.
- Voice AI fit: 0–25 — likely repetitive outbound/inbound conversation scale and measurable outcomes.
- Trigger/timing: 0–20 — freshness and strength of current business signals.
- Business value: 0–15 — plausible revenue, recovery, retention, service or cost impact.
- Buyer accessibility: 0–10 — identifiable relevant leaders/teams and public evidence.
- Strategic expansion fit: 0–5 — broader Pi orchestration potential.

Never score hidden facts as known. Reduce confidence when volumes/incumbents/budgets are unknown.

## Required output
For target-discovery requests return a ranked list (default top 20 unless seller asks otherwise) with:
- company;
- industry/segment;
- total score and confidence;
- CPaaS score;
- Voice AI score;
- strongest recent trigger(s) with date/source;
- why now;
- 1–3 likely use cases;
- likely buyer personas;
- named prospects when verifiable;
- current incumbent/provider only if verified;
- recommended land motion: CPaaS, Voice AI, both, or deprioritize;
- first outreach angle;
- next research/action.

Then group into Tier 1 / Tier 2 / Watchlist and explain the top five in more depth.

## Negative signals / deprioritization
Lower priority when there is weak customer interaction scale, no measurable conversational use case, no current trigger, unclear India relevance, poor buyer access, or the opportunity depends entirely on replacing a mature incumbent without evidence of dissatisfaction.

## Freshness
For 'today/latest/this week' use recent sources and time-stamp findings. For territory building, combine recent triggers with durable company fundamentals. Distinguish trigger date from source publication date where possible.

## Guardrails
Never invent company metrics, customer counts, message/call volumes, installed vendors, contracts, budgets, buying intent, executive priorities or contact details. Do not treat hiring or news as proof of an active procurement. Separate evidence, inference and unknowns.
