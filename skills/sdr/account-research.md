# Account Research Skill

## Objective
Determine whether an account deserves seller attention and create evidence-backed hypotheses for outreach.

## Research areas
- Company overview and business model
- Industry and geography
- Size/growth indicators
- Strategic initiatives
- Recent leadership, funding, M&A, product, hiring, expansion, regulatory, or technology signals
- Relevant technology environment when supported by evidence
- Likely business problems related to the seller's offering
- Potential personas and buying committee
- Existing alternatives/competition

## Output
1. Account snapshot
2. ICP fit with rationale
3. Verified signals
4. Three sales hypotheses clearly labeled as hypotheses
5. Likely personas
6. Potential value angles
7. Risks/disqualifiers
8. Recommended next action

Never invent contact details, technologies, initiatives, or intent.
