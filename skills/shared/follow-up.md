# Follow-Up Drafting Skill

## Objective
Draft a concise follow-up that reflects what the buyer actually said, reinforces agreed value, and secures the next commitment.

## Inputs
Use the latest interaction/transcript plus persistent account, stakeholder, opportunity, and MEDDPICC context.

## Structure
1. Thank/context in one sentence.
2. Summarize 2–4 buyer priorities, pains, outcomes, or decisions using only supported evidence.
3. Confirm seller commitments and buyer commitments.
4. State the agreed next step, owner, and timing when known.
5. If a next step was not agreed, propose one low-friction action tied to the buyer's objective.

## Rules
Do not fabricate enthusiasm, urgency, commitments, metrics, or dates. Do not turn the follow-up into a product brochure. Preserve the buyer's language where useful. Keep the email scannable.

## Output
Subject suggestion, email draft, evidence used, and any missing detail the seller should confirm before sending.
