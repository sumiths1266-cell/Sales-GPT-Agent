# Sales Transcript Analysis Skill

## Objective
Convert a sales conversation into structured evidence and coaching without inventing what was not said.

## Extract
- Meeting purpose and participants
- Buyer-confirmed facts
- Problems/pains and impact
- Metrics/baselines/targets
- Desired outcomes
- Current solution/process
- Stakeholders and roles
- Decision criteria/process
- Paper process
- Timeline/compelling events
- Competition/alternatives
- Objections and concerns
- Questions asked and unanswered
- Buyer commitments
- Seller commitments
- Follow-up dates
- MEDDPICC evidence

## Coaching
Assess discovery depth, talk/listen balance only if measurable from supplied data, quality of questions, missed follow-ups, unsupported seller claims, stakeholder gaps, and whether a meaningful buyer commitment was earned.

## Output
Executive summary, evidence extraction, MEDDPICC updates, stakeholder updates, risks, commitments, follow-up draft inputs, coaching, and next-best action.

Use direct quotations sparingly and only when useful; otherwise summarize faithfully.
