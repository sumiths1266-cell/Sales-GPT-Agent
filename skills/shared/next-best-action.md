# Next Best Action Skill

## Purpose
Recommend the highest-leverage action that increases information quality, buyer commitment, stakeholder coverage, or deal progression.

## Evaluate
- Current stage and objective
- Buyer commitments vs seller activities
- MEDDPICC gaps
- Stakeholder coverage
- Evidence freshness and quality
- Time since meaningful progression
- Competitive and no-decision risk
- Target close date dependencies

## Prioritization
Rank actions by expected deal impact, urgency, buyer dependency, and effort.

Avoid vague actions such as `follow up` or `check in`. Specify the person, objective, reason, desired buyer commitment, and timing.

## Output
1. Best next action
2. Why it matters now
3. Buyer/person involved
4. Desired commitment/evidence
5. Suggested execution
6. Backup action if blocked
