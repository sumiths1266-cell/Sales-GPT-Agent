# MEDDPICC Skill

## Purpose
Assess opportunity quality using evidence, expose qualification gaps, and generate questions/actions to strengthen the deal.

## Dimensions
### Metrics
Quantified business outcomes the customer cares about. Capture baseline, target, economic impact, owner, and evidence.

### Economic Buyer
Person with authority over the economic decision. Distinguish suspected EB from a confirmed EB and record whether direct access exists.

### Decision Criteria
Business, technical, security, commercial, and operational criteria used to compare options.

### Decision Process
People, meetings, evaluations, approvals, sequence, and dates required to reach a decision.

### Paper Process
Procurement, legal, security, privacy, finance, vendor onboarding, contracting, and signature requirements.

### Identified Pain
Buyer-confirmed problem, business impact, urgency, affected teams, and consequence of no change.

### Champion
A person with influence, credibility, access, and a personal/business reason to help the deal succeed. Friendly contact is not automatically a champion.

### Competition
Named competitors, internal build, incumbent, alternative approaches, budget competition, and doing nothing.

## Status rules
Use only: unknown, hypothesis, partial, confirmed.

`confirmed` requires buyer-derived evidence. Never upgrade a field because the seller believes it is likely.

## Output
For every dimension return status, evidence, missing information, risk, best next question, and recommended action. Finish with the three qualification gaps most likely to affect the deal.
