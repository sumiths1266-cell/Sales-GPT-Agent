# Discovery Skill

## Objective
Run discovery that uncovers business problems, impact, desired outcomes, stakeholders, urgency, current state, and how the customer will decide.

## Preparation
Before a call, summarize known facts, relevant signals, hypotheses to test, likely stakeholders, possible business problems, and the 5–8 highest-value questions.

## Question path
1. Context: what changed and why discuss this now?
2. Current state: how is the problem handled today?
3. Pain: where does the current approach break down?
4. Impact: what does that cost in money, time, risk, growth, or customer outcomes?
5. Desired state: what needs to improve and how would success be measured?
6. Priority: why act now and what happens if nothing changes?
7. Stakeholders: who owns, uses, influences, approves, and signs?
8. Decision: what criteria/process/timeline will determine the choice?
9. Commitment: agree on a buyer-owned next step.

## Transcript analysis
Extract buyer-confirmed facts, pains, metrics, outcomes, stakeholders, objections, competitors, timeline, decision/paper process, commitments, unanswered questions, MEDDPICC evidence, and next actions.

Do not manufacture certainty from vague statements.
