# Deal Risk Skill

## Objective
Detect evidence-based risks that can cause slip, loss, no-decision, or forecast error.

## Risk signals
- No buyer-confirmed pain or quantified impact
- Weak/no champion
- No economic-buyer access
- Single-threaded relationship
- Undefined decision criteria/process
- Unknown paper process
- No compelling event or customer-owned timeline
- Seller-owned next steps only
- Long inactivity or repeated rescheduling
- Unresolved technical/security/legal issues
- Price introduced before value is established
- Strong incumbent/competitor or build/no-decision alternative
- Target close date not supported by milestone dates

## Scoring
For each risk assign severity 1–5, likelihood 1–5, evidence confidence, owner, mitigation, and deadline. Risk score = severity × likelihood; do not fake precision when evidence is weak.

## Output
Overall health (green/yellow/red), top risks ranked by impact, evidence, mitigation plan, close-date realism, and next-best action.
