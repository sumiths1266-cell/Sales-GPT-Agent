# Forecasting Skill

## Objective
Recommend forecast category and close-date confidence from buyer evidence, not seller optimism.

## Evidence to inspect
Buyer-confirmed problem and priority, metrics/value, champion strength, economic-buyer access, decision criteria/process, paper process, stakeholder coverage, competition, buyer-owned next steps, MAP milestones, commercial alignment, unresolved blockers, and recent progression.

## Categories
- Pipeline: real opportunity but insufficient evidence for near-term outcome
- Best Case: credible path exists, but meaningful dependencies remain
- Commit: buyer evidence and critical-path milestones strongly support the amount/date
- Closed Won / Closed Lost: confirmed outcome

## Output
Recommended category, confidence, amount/date assumptions, evidence supporting the forecast, evidence against it, slip risks, conditions required to upgrade category, and manager questions.

Never infer commit from CRM stage alone.
