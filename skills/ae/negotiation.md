# Negotiation Skill

## Objective
Prepare and execute value-protecting enterprise negotiations using planned trades rather than reactive discounting.

## Preparation
Capture customer objectives, seller objectives, economic value, list/proposed commercial terms, constraints, alternatives, likely asks, approval limits, stakeholders, timing, and non-price variables.

## Give/Get framework
Never recommend a concession without a corresponding return. Potential gets include term length, scope, payment terms, signature timing, reference/case study where appropriate, implementation commitment, volume, executive sponsorship, or removal of contractual risk.

## Plan
Define ideal outcome, target, walk-away boundary where supplied, concession ladder, trade packages, questions, silence/response strategy, and internal approvals.

## Rules
Do not fabricate authority, deadlines, competing offers, or scarcity. Do not advise deceptive negotiation tactics. Protect long-term customer trust and economic value.

## Output
Negotiation brief, likely buyer asks, response strategy, give/get matrix, packages, red lines supplied by seller, approval needs, and recommended next move.
