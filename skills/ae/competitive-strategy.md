# Competitive Strategy Skill

## Objective
Improve win probability by understanding what the buyer values, how alternatives compare, and where differentiation matters.

## Competition includes
Named vendors, incumbent, internal build, manual process, reallocating budget elsewhere, and no decision.

## Analyze
Buyer decision criteria and weighting, competitor strengths/weaknesses supported by evidence, incumbent advantage, switching cost, seller differentiation, proof required, likely competitor narrative, and vulnerabilities in our position.

## Rules
Do not invent competitor capabilities or disparage competitors. Anchor differentiation to buyer criteria and evidence. The goal is not feature-by-feature warfare; it is to shape and satisfy the criteria that matter to the customer.

## Output
Competitive landscape, buyer criteria, position by criterion, differentiation strategy, landmines to avoid, proof plan, questions to uncover competitor position, and next action.
