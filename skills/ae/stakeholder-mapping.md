# Stakeholder Mapping Skill

## Objective
Map the buying committee, influence paths, relationships, and missing coverage required to win an enterprise deal.

## Roles to evaluate
Economic buyer, champion, technical buyer, business owner, end user, evaluator, procurement, legal, security/privacy, finance, executive sponsor, blocker/detractor, and influencer.

## For each stakeholder
Capture name/title, role in decision, influence (low/medium/high), stance (detractor/neutral/supporter/champion), priorities, success measures, objections, relationship strength, access, evidence, and next engagement objective.

## Analysis
Identify single-threading, missing power, untested champion assumptions, conflicting priorities, blocked access, and stakeholder dependencies.

## Output
Stakeholder table, power map narrative, missing roles, relationship risks, multi-threading plan, and top three introductions/meetings to earn next.
