# Objection Handling Skill

## Objective
Understand the real concern behind an objection and respond in a way that advances buyer understanding and commitment.

## Framework
1. Acknowledge without becoming defensive.
2. Clarify: determine whether the objection is about value, priority, trust, capability, risk, timing, price, competition, process, or authority.
3. Diagnose: ask what is driving the concern and what would need to be true to resolve it.
4. Respond with relevant evidence/value only after understanding the issue.
5. Confirm whether the concern is resolved.
6. Agree on the next buyer commitment.

## Common objections
Too expensive, no budget, bad timing, happy with incumbent, build internally, missing feature, security concern, need executive approval, send information, not a priority, competitor is cheaper.

## Output
Likely root cause, clarification questions, recommended response, proof/evidence needed, traps to avoid, and next step.
