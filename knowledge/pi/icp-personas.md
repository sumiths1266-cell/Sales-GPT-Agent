# Pi — ICP & Persona Playbook

## Priority industries
Public Pi industry solutions currently emphasize BFSI, Retail, Hospitality and E-Commerce. Adjacent businesses can qualify when they have high customer volumes, measurable conversion/collection/service outcomes and repeatable journeys.

## Enterprise ICP signals
Prioritize accounts with several of these signals:
- Large lead, customer, borrower, policyholder, shopper, merchant or guest volumes.
- High-volume outbound/inbound customer conversations.
- Material spend on performance marketing or customer acquisition.
- Cart/lead abandonment, churn, collections, renewals, COD/RTO, support or repeat-purchase problems.
- Multiple channels or fragmented CRM/MarTech/customer-service workflows.
- Outcomes measurable in revenue, conversion, recovery, retention, cost-to-serve or operational efficiency.

Do not mark an account high-fit solely because it is a famous brand or large company.

## Persona map

### Growth / Marketing / Digital
Typical titles: CMO, VP/Head of Marketing, Growth, Digital, Performance Marketing, CRM, Lifecycle, Retention.
Lead with: conversion, CAC efficiency, lead recovery, campaign execution, retention, cross-sell/upsell and revenue attribution.

### Customer Experience / Service
Typical titles: CXO, Head of Customer Experience, Customer Service, Contact Centre, Operations.
Lead with: query resolution, proactive service, automation, handoff, cost-to-serve and customer experience.

### BFSI Collections / Lending / Insurance
Typical titles: Head of Collections, Lending, Credit Operations, Renewals, Insurance Operations, Digital Collections.
Lead with: payment recovery, promise-to-pay, renewals, failed-payment journeys and scalable customer conversations.

### E-commerce / Commerce / Operations
Typical titles: Head of E-commerce, Digital Commerce, Marketplace, Operations, CRM, Retention.
Lead with: cart recovery, checkout completion, COD verification, RTO reduction, reorder and win-back.

### Technology / Data / Security
Typical titles: CIO, CTO, Engineering, Enterprise Architecture, Data, Security.
Engage when integration, data flow, governance, reliability, AI architecture or deployment becomes part of the decision. Do not make them the default economic buyer without evidence.

## Qualification principle
Map the persona to a measurable business outcome. Product interest without an owner, business problem, scale and success metric is not qualified demand.
