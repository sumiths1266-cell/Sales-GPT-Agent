# Paytm / Pi — Voice AI Land-Motion Playbook

## Commercial priority
Treat Voice AI as a primary entry product. Start with high-volume, repetitive, measurable calling journeys where AI can create business value. Broader Pi orchestration is the expansion vision.

## High-fit use cases
- Dormant customer/member reactivation.
- Lead qualification and follow-up.
- Renewal and retention conversations.
- Collections/payment reminders and promise-to-pay capture.
- Service/status/outbound information journeys.
- Feedback/surveys where conversational voice is appropriate.

## Discovery
- Monthly call attempts, connected calls and peak concurrency.
- Human-agent vs automated call mix.
- Answer/connect rate, conversion/recovery/reactivation and AHT.
- Cost per call, successful contact and completed outcome.
- Languages and customer cohorts.
- Top intents, objections and call dispositions.
- Required CRM/source-system actions after a call.
- Human handoff/escalation rules.
- Recording, consent, audit, compliance and quiet-hour requirements.
- Current dialer/contact-centre/voice vendor and pain points.

## Qualification
Strong opportunities have scale + repetitive conversations + measurable outcome + acceptable automation scope + a business owner. Do not qualify solely because the company has a contact centre.

## Positioning
Voice AI is not the business outcome. Position it as a scalable conversational execution layer that can understand customer intent/disposition and move an approved journey toward reactivation, qualification, payment, renewal or resolution.

## ROI framework
Use buyer baselines. Compare cost and performance at the level of successful outcome, not only cost per call. Never invent uplift, accuracy, latency, implementation time or savings.

## Objections
Human touch: identify journeys/cohorts where human agents add the most value and where AI can handle repetitive first-line conversations.
AI quality: define evaluation, controls, escalation and proof criteria.
Existing dialer/BPO: explore coexistence and incremental use cases before suggesting replacement.

## Expansion
Land one measurable voice journey → prove business outcome → expand cohorts/use cases → connect Voice AI with WhatsApp/CPaaS and broader Pi orchestration where useful.
