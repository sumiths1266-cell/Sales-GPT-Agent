# Pi by Paytm — Omnichannel Commerce & Customer Engagement Knowledge

## Primary positioning
Pi Marketing is an AI-powered omnichannel customer engagement and commerce platform built to move customers from contact to completed business outcomes.

Do not position Pi primarily as a Voice AI product. Voice AI is one channel/capability inside a broader orchestration layer.

Core mental model:

Customer data → omnichannel outreach → AI conversation/orchestration → completion action → measurable outcome → CRM/MarTech update.

## Commerce and engagement channel layer
Pi should be sold around coordinated customer journeys across communication channels, with the exact production availability confirmed for the customer's deployment.

### WhatsApp
A core journey channel in the enterprise deck. Use for conversational outreach, follow-ups, nudges, payment/purchase links, checkout completion, collections reminders and service interactions.

### Voice AI
An integrated conversational channel, especially useful for high-intent/high-value cohorts, collections, retention, service and journeys where voice improves contactability. Voice can branch into WhatsApp or other steps based on disposition.

### Chat AI
Conversational AI for two-way customer interactions, intent capture, resolution and continuity across journeys where supported.

### SMS / RCS / Email
Treat these as important omnichannel commerce/engagement channels in Pi's strategic product model. The June 2026 enterprise deck places 'More Channels: RCS, SMS, Email' in the product roadmap section, so do not present them as generally live/current in a customer deployment without current internal confirmation.

## Orchestration is the product
The seller should not frame Pi as 'we send WhatsApp/voice/messages.' The platform coordinates channels and actions toward an outcome.

Key orchestration concepts from the enterprise deck:
- audience ingestion via CRM, CSV or API;
- visual and prompt-based journey creation;
- channel sequencing and switching;
- disposition/intent-driven branching;
- automatic fallback when a channel does not connect/convert;
- A/B testing of message variants and journey flows;
- integrated Voice + Chat AI agents;
- payment, purchase, lead-completion or service actions inside the journey;
- outcome tracking and CRM/MarTech sync;
- human approval gates and auditability.

## Example commerce journey
Cart abandoned → WhatsApp recovery → no conversion → high-value cohort receives AI Voice → interested disposition → checkout/payment link → purchase tracked → CRM/MarTech updated.

This is a journey hypothesis, not a mandatory sequence. Channel order should depend on customer segment, consent, economics, use case and measured performance.

## Example collections journey
Delinquent audience ingested → WhatsApp reminder → disposition-based branch → Voice AI for selected cohorts → payment link → payment tracked → follow-up/fallback → source system updated.

## Customer lifecycle outcomes
### Acquire
Reach/activate prospects and customers through relevant channels and campaigns.
### Convert
Move intent toward lead completion, purchase, payment or another buyer-defined CTA.
### Collect
Drive reminders, promise-to-pay and payment completion journeys.
### Service
Resolve or progress customer needs with AI conversation and controlled human escalation.
### Retain & grow
Reactivation, loyalty, cross-sell, upsell, replenishment and win-back journeys.

## How to position against adjacent categories
### CPaaS
CPaaS often solves channel delivery/infrastructure. Pi's selling question is what happens after delivery: conversation, branching, action, outcome and measurement. Never imply CPaaS quality is unimportant.

### CEP / MarTech
CEP platforms may own segmentation, campaigns and lifecycle orchestration. Pi can be evaluated as an execution/conversation/completion layer and may coexist with CRM/MarTech rather than replace it.

### Conversational / Voice AI
Move the discussion from a single bot/channel to the complete customer journey: audience, channel orchestration, conversation, business action, outcome tracking and feedback.

## Proof points from the enterprise deck
Use approved Paytm internal proof points when relevant, with exact numbers and context preserved from the source deck. Do not generalize internal Paytm outcomes into guaranteed external customer outcomes.

## Live vs roadmap guardrail
The June 2026 enterprise deck demonstrates WhatsApp, Voice AI, Chat AI, journey orchestration, Ask Pi, branching/fallbacks, analytics and human approval concepts as current product positioning. It separately lists RCS, SMS and Email under 'More Channels' in the roadmap.

Before a customer meeting, confirm current status of roadmap capabilities with the latest internal product material because channel availability can change.

## Seller rule
Sell the business journey and measurable outcome first, then show how Pi orchestrates channels to achieve it. Never reduce Pi to messaging volume, WhatsApp APIs or Voice AI alone. Never invent channel availability, integrations, pricing, performance, compliance commitments or implementation timelines.
