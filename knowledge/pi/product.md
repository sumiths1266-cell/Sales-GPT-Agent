# Pi by Paytm — Product Sales Knowledge

Source of truth for public product claims: https://pi.global/

## Positioning
Pi Agents is Paytm's AI-agent growth platform. Position it around completed business outcomes rather than messaging volume: acquire customers, communicate, convert, collect, resolve service needs, recover carts, and grow repeat revenue.

## Agent portfolio

### Pi Marketing Agent
Outcomes: acquire, communicate, convert, collect.
Capabilities publicly described by Pi include CRM/CSV/API audience ingestion; Voice AI, WhatsApp, RCS and SMS outreach; intelligent two-way dialogue; ad planning/launch/optimisation; payment or purchase completion actions; CRM/MarTech sync; and execution across WhatsApp, SMS, RCS, Chat AI, Paytm Ads, Voice AI, Meta Ads, web/app and Google Ads.

### Pi Collections Agent
Outcomes: reminders, payment and recovery.
Use cases include EMI and premium reminders, promise-to-pay and renewal commitment capture, collections/renewal queries, chargeback disputes, and updating loan/policy systems.

### Pi Service Agent
Outcome: query resolution and retention.
Use cases include real-time query resolution, transaction/status communication, refund and complaint requests, delivery/rescheduling coordination and human escalation when required.

### Pi Commerce Agent
Outcomes: cart recovery, address verification and delivered orders.
Use cases include cart-abandonment recovery, WhatsApp/SMS nudges, COD address verification, order/delivery confirmation and order-management-system sync.

## Platform credibility
Public Pi positioning says Paytm has deployed 100+ Voice AI and Chat AI agents across its own businesses and describes infrastructure including Meta BSP status, Google/AWS partnerships and Paytm's telecom network. Pi positions its AI as sovereign and designed for India and the Middle East, including DPDP-oriented design and native Hindi and Arabic support.

## Seller rule
Never turn a capability into a guaranteed customer result. Treat performance numbers, customer logos, case-study outcomes and infrastructure statistics as externally verifiable claims and confirm current wording on pi.global before using them with a buyer.
