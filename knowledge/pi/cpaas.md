# Paytm / Pi — CPaaS Land-Motion Playbook

## Commercial priority
Treat CPaaS as a primary entry product when researching, prospecting and qualifying accounts. The broader Pi platform is an expansion story unless the buyer's stated need clearly calls for it.

## What to discover
- Current channels: WhatsApp, SMS, RCS, email and other messaging relevant to the buyer.
- Current provider(s) and what they do well.
- Monthly/peak message volumes by channel and use case.
- Transactional vs promotional/service traffic.
- Delivery, read/engagement and conversion metrics where available.
- Reliability, throughput, latency, support and reporting requirements.
- Template/campaign operations and API/integration model.
- Pricing/commercial model and cost sensitivity.
- Consent, DLT/regulatory, frequency and governance requirements.
- Whether channels are isolated or coordinated in customer journeys.

## Qualification
A large messaging volume alone is not a qualified opportunity. Find a buyer-owned reason to evaluate: cost, reliability, reach, support, consolidation, new channel/use case, orchestration, integration, commercial terms or incumbent dissatisfaction.

## Buyer personas
Marketing/Growth/CRM/Lifecycle for engagement use cases; CX/Service/Operations for service communication; Product/Digital for customer journeys; Engineering/Platform for APIs and reliability; Procurement/Finance for commercials. Identify the actual owner from evidence.

## Positioning
Lead with the customer's communication use case and measurable outcome. Do not reduce the pitch to message pricing. Where relevant, connect CPaaS to the future ability to add AI conversation and orchestration through Pi.

## Competitive rule
Never invent the incumbent CPaaS. If unknown, ask. Compare providers against buyer-confirmed criteria rather than generic battlecards.

## Expansion
Land with a channel/use case → prove delivery/economics/operations → expand channels and use cases → introduce Voice AI or broader Pi orchestration where there is a business case.
