# Pi — Outcome-Based Sales Plays

Use these as hypotheses to investigate, not claims that every account has the problem.

## Lead recovery & conversion
Trigger signals: high paid acquisition, lead forms, app funnels, sales-assisted conversion, slow follow-up.
Hypothesis: valuable leads are leaking between acquisition and completion because follow-up is delayed, generic or fragmented.
Relevant Pi motion: Marketing Agent with conversational outreach, qualification/follow-up and completion action.
Proof metric: lead-to-qualified, lead-to-sale, time-to-first-response, recovered revenue.

## Retention / cross-sell / win-back
Signals: large customer base, repeatable purchases/financial products, lifecycle CRM investment.
Hypothesis: customer data exists but journeys do not consistently turn intent into conversations and completed purchases.
Relevant motion: Marketing Agent; industry-specific retention/cross-sell journeys.
Proof metric: repeat purchase, reactivation, cross-sell conversion, incremental revenue.

## Collections / renewals
Signals: lender/NBFC/insurance/brokerage, large recurring dues or renewals.
Hypothesis: manual and one-way reminders limit contactability and completion while increasing servicing cost.
Relevant motion: Collections Agent.
Proof metric: contactability, promise-to-pay, payment/recovery, renewal, cost per recovered account.

## Service automation
Signals: large contact centre, repetitive intents, high support volume, status/refund/delivery questions.
Hypothesis: repetitive interactions consume human capacity that could be automated while preserving escalation for complex cases.
Relevant motion: Service Agent.
Proof metric: resolution/containment, AHT, repeat contact, escalation, cost-to-serve, CSAT where available.

## Cart recovery / COD / RTO
Signals: D2C/e-commerce, COD exposure, logistics complexity, meaningful abandoned-cart volume.
Hypothesis: revenue leaks after product intent because checkout/cart recovery and delivery verification are not conversational or timely enough.
Relevant motion: Commerce Agent.
Proof metric: recovered carts, checkout completion, address verification, RTO, delivered-order rate, recovered GMV/revenue.

## Local/store growth
Signals: multi-location retail/hospitality, local demand variability, loyalty/CRM opportunity.
Hypothesis: stores/locations have customer data and local demand but lack automated hyperlocal acquisition and repeat-visit activation.
Relevant motion: Pi's retail/hospitality growth use cases.
Proof metric: walk-ins/orders, repeat visits, redemption, campaign-attributed revenue.
