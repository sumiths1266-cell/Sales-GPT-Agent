# Pi — Enterprise Discovery Playbook

## Goal
Discover whether Pi can materially improve a buyer-owned business outcome. Do not run a feature checklist.

## Core discovery sequence
1. Business objective — What outcome must improve this quarter/year?
2. Current journey — How does the customer journey work today from trigger to completion?
3. Scale — Leads/customers/conversations/orders/due payments/tickets and relevant frequency.
4. Baseline — Current conversion, recovery, contactability, abandonment, RTO, renewal, retention, response or service metrics.
5. Friction — Where does the journey fail, slow down or require manual work?
6. Economics — What is the revenue leakage, acquisition waste, recovery gap or operating cost?
7. Existing stack — CRM, CDP, MarTech, contact centre, messaging, ad platforms, order/loan/policy systems and integrations.
8. AI readiness — What is automated today? What requires human review? What governance constraints exist?
9. Decision — Who owns the metric, who signs, who validates technology/security, and how will a vendor be selected?
10. Success — What measurable pilot/production outcome would justify expansion?

## Use-case branches

### Acquisition / Marketing
Ask about paid-media spend, lead sources, lead response time, qualification, drop-off, CRM journeys, channel mix, campaign operations, conversion attribution and cost per acquired customer.

### Collections / Renewals
Ask about portfolio/due volume, contactability, reminder cadence, promise-to-pay, failed payments, agent workload, cure/recovery rates, channels, compliance and system updates.

### Service
Ask about ticket/contact volume, top intents, containment/resolution, AHT, repeat contacts, proactive status needs, escalation rules and cost-to-serve.

### Commerce
Ask about cart abandonment, checkout drop-off, COD mix, address failures, RTO, delivery communication, reorder cycles, win-back and revenue lost at each step.

## MEDDPICC mapping
Metrics = baseline + target outcome.
Economic buyer = owner of budget/business outcome, supported by evidence.
Decision criteria = business, technical, security and commercial requirements.
Decision process = actual steps/people/timing.
Paper process = procurement/legal/security/commercial path.
Identified pain = quantified or operationally demonstrated problem.
Champion = person with influence, urgency and willingness to sell internally.
Competition = vendors plus status quo, internal build and 'do nothing'.

## Guardrail
If a metric is unknown, ask for it. Never invent conversion lifts, ROI, savings, implementation time or customer results.
