# Pi — Objection Handling

Use acknowledge → diagnose → respond → verify. Never attack the incumbent or invent unsupported differentiation.

## "We already have a CPaaS / messaging vendor"
Diagnose whether the buyer sees the problem as message delivery or completed business outcomes. Ask what happens after delivery: response understanding, two-way conversation, next action, conversion/payment/resolution and system update.
Position Pi around AI agents and outcome workflows where relevant, while confirming integration and channel requirements before claiming fit.

## "We already have a CRM / MarTech platform"
Do not position Pi as automatically replacing it. Pi publicly describes CRM/MarTech ingestion and sync. Explore whether the current stack can autonomously execute the conversation and completion journey the buyer wants.

## "We can build AI ourselves"
Explore scope, time-to-production, channel infrastructure, model operations, integrations, governance, measurement and ongoing optimisation. Compare build vs buy using the buyer's resources and requirements; do not assert a universal answer.

## "AI quality / hallucination worries us"
Ask which journeys are acceptable for automation, what actions require deterministic controls/human handoff, and what accuracy/audit requirements exist. Bring technical/product specialists into validation rather than promising zero hallucinations.

## "Security / compliance / data residency"
Treat as a validation workstream. Pi publicly positions itself as DPDP-compliant by design and sovereign AI for India/Middle East, but exact architecture, certifications, data handling, retention and contractual commitments must be confirmed with current official documentation/product/security teams.

## "No budget"
Determine whether there is truly no budget or no quantified business case. Reconnect to revenue leakage, recovery opportunity, acquisition waste or operating cost. Do not fabricate ROI.

## "Send a proposal"
Do not jump straight to pricing when success criteria are unclear. Confirm use case, volume, current baseline, target, channels, integrations, stakeholders, buying process and evaluation criteria first.
