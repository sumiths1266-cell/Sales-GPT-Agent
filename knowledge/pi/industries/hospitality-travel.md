# Pi Industry Playbook — Hospitality & Travel

## Pi fit
Pi publicly positions hospitality around Order & Pay, Advertise, Market, and Loyalty & Redemption. Relevant broader Pi capabilities also support service conversations, confirmations, status updates and rescheduling. For travel-specific use cases, validate fit rather than assuming hospitality features apply identically.

## Segments
Hotels/resorts, restaurant/QSR chains, travel platforms, airlines and other high-volume booking/guest businesses. Pi's public industry page explicitly details hospitality; travel/airline plays below are hypotheses requiring account-specific validation.

## Personas
Marketing/Growth, Digital, Revenue, CRM/Loyalty, Customer Experience, Reservations/Contact Centre, Operations, E-commerce/Direct Channels.

## Hospitality plays
### Direct order / booking conversion
Explore QR/storefront/voice ordering or direct digital conversion where relevant.
Metrics: conversion, direct-order share, abandoned journey recovery, revenue.
### Demand generation
Metrics: campaign conversion, bookings/orders, CAC/ROAS, off-peak demand.
### CRM & loyalty
Metrics: repeat visit/stay/order, redemption, reactivation, direct revenue, LTV.
### Guest service
Metrics: resolution, response time, repeat contacts, human escalation, cost-to-serve.

## Travel hypotheses
Booking abandonment recovery, payment completion, itinerary/status communication, rescheduling/service, loyalty/reactivation and ancillary cross-sell may be relevant. Validate product support, integration and buyer priority before presenting as a Pi solution.

## Triggers
New properties/routes/locations, loyalty launch, direct-booking push, seasonal/off-peak demand, contact-centre pressure, digital transformation, app/site redesign, expansion into new markets.

## Discovery
What share of demand is direct vs aggregator/partner? Where does booking/order conversion drop? What does acquisition cost? What customer/guest data exists? Repeat frequency and loyalty penetration? Top service intents? Which booking/PMS/POS/CRM/contact-centre systems matter? What outcome would justify a pilot?

## Expansion
Acquisition/demand → conversion/order → loyalty/reactivation → service → ancillary/cross-sell where validated.
