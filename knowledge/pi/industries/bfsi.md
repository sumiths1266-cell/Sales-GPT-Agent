# Pi Industry Playbook — BFSI

## Pi fit
Pi publicly positions BFSI around Acquire, Convert, Collect and Communicate: lead generation/qualification/onboarding including KYC/KYB; cross-sell such as payments-to-credit, borrowers-to-insurance and SIP expansion; EMI/premium reminders, promise-to-pay, UPI Autopay, failed-payment retry and chargebacks; and reactive/proactive customer communication.

## Segments
Banks, NBFCs, fintech/lending, insurance, brokerage/investments and payments businesses.

## Priority business problems
- Paid/partner leads fail to activate or complete onboarding.
- Application/KYC journeys drop before activation.
- Existing customers have underpenetrated products.
- EMI/premium/renewal recovery requires high manual effort.
- Failed payments and promises-to-pay are not converted into completion.
- Service/status interactions consume human capacity.

## Triggers to research
Growth in loan/card/insurance/investment products; new digital products; branch/contact-centre expansion; collections hiring; AI/customer-experience initiatives; rising acquisition investment; partnerships; regulatory/customer-service initiatives. Treat each as a signal, not proof of pain.

## Personas
Business: Head of Lending, Cards, Insurance, Investments, Growth, Digital, Collections, Customer Experience.
Operations: Collections Ops, Loan Ops, Insurance Ops, Contact Centre.
Technical validators: CIO/CTO, architecture, data, security, compliance/risk.
Potential economic buyer: business owner of the target metric; verify rather than infer from title.

## Plays
### Acquisition & activation
Hypothesis: leads leak between acquisition, qualification, KYC/KYB and activation.
Metrics: lead-to-application, KYC completion, approval-to-activation, time-to-contact, CAC.

### Cross-sell
Hypothesis: existing customer intent/data is not consistently converted into additional products.
Metrics: eligible-to-offer, offer-to-conversion, product/customer, incremental revenue.

### Collections & renewals
Hypothesis: one-way reminders/manual calling limit contactability and payment completion.
Metrics: contactability, PTP, PTP kept, recovery/cure, renewal, cost per recovered account.

### Service
Hypothesis: repetitive status/payment/refund/query interactions can be resolved automatically with controlled escalation.
Metrics: containment/resolution, AHT, repeat contact, escalation, cost-to-serve.

## Discovery
What portfolio/journey are we improving? What is monthly volume? Baseline conversion/recovery? Where is leakage? Which customer cohorts matter? What channels work today? What actions can an AI agent execute? Which core/loan/policy/CRM systems need updates? What compliance/consent/audit rules apply? Who owns the metric and budget? What pilot result would justify rollout?

## Objections / proof
Security, compliance, data handling, model quality, customer consent and integration are validation workstreams. Do not promise certifications or regulatory acceptance. Bring Pi product/security teams into proof.

## Expansion
Land on one measurable journey, then expand across acquisition → activation → cross-sell → collections/renewal → service when evidence supports it.
