# Pi Industry Playbook — E-Commerce / D2C

## Pi fit
Pi publicly positions e-commerce around Acquire, Convert, Fulfil and Retain: performance ads/catalogue creative; cart recovery and checkout nudges; real-time upsell; COD address verification, delivery updates and RTO intervention; reorder/replenishment, reviews and win-back.

## ICP signals
Meaningful paid acquisition, high site/app traffic, repeatable purchase categories, material abandoned carts, COD exposure, large customer database, lifecycle/CRM investment, logistics complexity or frequent promotions.

## Personas
Head/VP E-commerce, Growth, Performance Marketing, CRM/Lifecycle, Retention, Digital, Revenue, Customer Experience, Operations/Fulfilment. Technical/data teams validate integrations.

## Plays
### Paid acquisition → conversion
Metrics: CAC, ROAS, click-to-purchase, lead/product-view-to-order, revenue.
### Cart/checkout recovery
Metrics: abandonment, recovery rate, checkout completion, recovered GMV/revenue.
### COD / RTO
Metrics: address confirmation, cancellation, RTO, delivered-order rate, logistics cost.
### Retention
Metrics: reorder, repeat purchase, reactivation, LTV, cross-sell/upsell.
### Service
Metrics: resolution, order-status contacts, refund contacts, cost-to-serve.

## Triggers
Funding/growth push, new categories/geographies, major sale season, rising ad investment, retention hiring, loyalty launch, logistics/RTO focus, app relaunch, omnichannel expansion. Verify pain before pitching.

## Discovery
Where does revenue leak from ad click through delivered order? What are traffic/order/cart volumes? Abandonment and recovery baseline? COD share and RTO? Repeat purchase cycle? Current WhatsApp/SMS/email journeys? CRM/CDP/commerce/OMS stack? Who owns conversion vs fulfilment vs retention? What incremental revenue or cost reduction makes a pilot meaningful?

## Outreach angle
Use an observable funnel/commerce trigger and one leakage hypothesis. Avoid generic 'AI for e-commerce'. Example structure: signal → likely funnel moment → measurable outcome → question.

## Objections
Existing CEP: determine whether Pi complements journey orchestration with conversational completion.
Existing CPaaS: distinguish channel delivery from business action without dismissing infrastructure.
Internal automation: compare against full workflow, integrations, measurement and operations.

## Expansion
Cart recovery → checkout/upsell → COD/RTO → reorder/win-back → acquisition/service, depending on demonstrated ROI.
