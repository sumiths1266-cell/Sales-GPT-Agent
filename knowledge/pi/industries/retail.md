# Pi Industry Playbook — Retail

## Pi fit
Pi publicly positions retail around Acquire, Retain, Relate and Grow: lookalike-led ads across Paytm/Google/Meta with AI creative; loyalty, vouchers/cashback, occasion/calendar triggers and win-back; WhatsApp invoices, two-way conversations and reviews; cross-sell, upsell, replenishment, subscription and referrals.

## ICP signals
Multi-store or omnichannel footprint, loyalty/customer database, frequent purchases, seasonal demand, local marketing needs, high walk-in dependence, store-level acquisition goals or repeat-purchase opportunity.

## Personas
CMO/Marketing, Head of Retail, Digital/Omnichannel, CRM/Loyalty, Growth, E-commerce, Customer Experience, Regional/Store Operations.

## Plays
### Store acquisition / footfall
Metrics: attributed walk-ins/orders, cost per acquisition, campaign conversion, local revenue.
### Loyalty & win-back
Metrics: enrolment, active members, repeat rate, redemption, churn reactivation, LTV.
### Customer conversation
Metrics: response, invoice/service completion, reviews/ratings, resolution.
### Basket growth
Metrics: cross-sell/upsell conversion, basket value, replenishment, subscription/referral revenue.

## Triggers
New stores/cities, festive season, loyalty revamp, omnichannel strategy, new category, declining footfall, store marketing decentralisation, customer-data initiative.

## Discovery
How are stores acquiring local customers today? Can purchases be linked to customer identity? Loyalty penetration? Repeat frequency? How are local campaigns launched? What customer data is available? What is the current win-back process? Which POS/CRM/loyalty/e-commerce systems matter? Which metric is owned centrally vs store/region?

## Proof strategy
Choose a bounded geography/cohort/use case with measurable baseline and attributable business outcome. Avoid claiming footfall/revenue lift without controlled evidence.

## Expansion
Local acquisition → loyalty/win-back → cross-sell/replenishment → omnichannel service/commerce.
